About StudentHub

A student registration website built with the Django framework for Assignment 19.

StudentHub demonstrates how a Django project is organized into projects, apps, views, URLs and templates — and how a ModelForm turns a plain HTML form into rows in a real database.

Every registration submitted through the Register page is validated server-side and stored in SQLite. The Students page reads those rows back and renders them as a roster.

Project & app

The config project holds settings and root URLs; the pages app holds views, forms, models and templates.

Views & URLs

Function-based views are mapped to named routes like /register/ and /students/.

Models & forms

The Student model persists registrations; a ModelForm generates and validates its fields.
